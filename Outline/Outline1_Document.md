## Outline for Your Book on Health Fundamentals

### Introduction
- Explain why modern health advice feels fragmented and why fundamentals still matter.
- Promise a practical, whole-food-centered framework readers can apply without perfectionism.

### Chapter 1: The Building Blocks of Health
- Cover water, minerals, vitamins, protein, and essential fats as the foundational supports.
- Show that whole foods often bring several of these together.

### Chapter 2: How the Body Works Together
- Explain synergy, cofactors, gut-brain dynamics, and system-wide relationships.
- Help readers understand why isolated strategies often fall short.

### Chapter 3: Nutrient Delivery and Practical Applications
- Turn the framework into practical delivery through blending, juicing, and better combinations.
- Emphasize bioavailability, repeatability, and real-life support.

### Chapter 4: Pillars of Vitality
- Expand into sleep, movement, calm, rhythm, and mindset.
- Show how lifestyle affects the body's ability to use support well.

### Chapter 5: Affordable and Natural Health Solutions
- Highlight simple high-value foods, smarter shopping, practical prep, and realistic kitchen habits.
- Reinforce dignity and usability in ordinary food.

### Chapter 6: The Missing Instruction Manual
- Explain the extraction problem, the food matrix, and why isolated products can disappoint.
- Re-establish the hierarchy of foundation first and specialized support second.

### Chapter 7: The Common Food Superpower
- Restore dignity to common foods, mineral-rich basics, and everyday grocery-store support.
- Show how mineralization happens through ordinary combinations.

### Chapter 8: The Blender Bridge
- Present blending as a practical transition away from dependence on bottles.
- Cover flavor, prep, convenience, and the lived difference between blending and juicing.

### Chapter 9: Detoxing Your Life: Reducing Hidden Burdens
- Address avoidable burdens in the body, home, schedule, and relationships.
- Focus on reduction, relief, and lower interference.

### Chapter 10: Sustaining Health Over Time
- Teach return, maintenance, accountability, and resilience after setbacks.
- Replace perfectionism with durable, repeatable patterns.

### Chapter 11: The Role of AI and the Future of Health
- Help readers use technology wisely without surrendering judgment.
- End with agency, discernment, and fundamentals still at the center.
