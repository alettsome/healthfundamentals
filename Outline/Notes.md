#### **Unique Framework: The Health Fundamentals Triage**
- **Purpose**: To provide a simple, memorable structure for understanding and applying health fundamentals.
- **Framework Overview**:
  1. **Foundation**: The essential building blocks of health (e.g., vitamins, minerals, amino acids, enzymes, and water).
     - Focus: Understanding the basics and their roles in the body.
  2. **Integration**: How the body works together (e.g., nutrient synergy, gut-brain connection, nervous system regulation).
     - Focus: Highlighting the interdependence of nutrients and systems.
  3. **Application**: Practical steps for daily health (e.g., balanced diet, lifestyle choices, affordable solutions).
     - Focus: Translating knowledge into actionable habits.
- **Visual Representation**: A triage-inspired diagram showing the progression from foundational knowledge to practical application.
- **Integration into the Book**: Each part of the book aligns with a tier of the triage, ensuring clarity and consistency.
