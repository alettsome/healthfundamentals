# Marketing Workspace

This folder holds the book's early marketing outputs.

It sits downstream from:

- `Business/Packaging/FinalPackagingSet.md`
- `Business/MarketingMessageStarter.md`

Turn the business plan and packaging work into usable reader-facing marketing language.

## Recommended Use

Use this folder for:

- short promotional hooks
- launch messaging
- outreach copy
- retailer-facing copy variations
- ad or social copy starters
- reader-problem-to-book-promise messaging
