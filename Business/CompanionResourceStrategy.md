# Companion Resource Strategy

## Purpose

Define the strongest buyer-only companion resource for *Health Fundamentals* and how it should work.

## Recommendation

The strongest companion resource is:

## Health Fundamentals Companion Field Guide

Why this is the best fit:

- practical
- fast to use
- clearly connected to the main book
- valuable without replacing the book
- easy to deliver digitally
- useful for both direct buyers and Amazon buyers
- broader and stronger than a narrow quick-start booklet

## What It Should Do

It should help readers move from:

- understanding

to:

- first action

quickly.

It should not feel like a second full book.
It should feel like a clear bridge into action.

## Best Content Structure

### 1. Start Here

- what to focus on first
- what not to overcomplicate
- how to begin without panic

### 2. Your First Food Swaps

- simple first replacements
- affordable starting points
- a few high-value staples
- a low-friction first week approach

### 3. What To Reduce First

- obvious friction
- obvious overload
- obvious derailers

### 4. The Simple Blender Builder

- hydration base
- protein anchor
- mineral-rich addition
- fat support
- flavor upgrade

### 5. Tight-Week Buying Guide

- what to buy when money is low
- what matters most
- what can wait

### 6. Produce Cleaning And Basic Kitchen Habits

- simple washing approach
- low-cost preparation habits
- practical storage and prep guidance

### 7. Blender Buying Guide

- what matters most
- what is optional
- how to choose by budget, household size, and use pattern
- how to avoid paying for marketing noise

### 8. Reset After A Hard Week

- what to do first
- how to recover without starting over

### 9. Field Pages

Avoid formal workbook language.

Use light practical pages such as:

- food-swap map
- weekly reset page
- pantry check page
- reflection prompts

## Suggested Size

Best size:

- 12 to 20 pages

Why:

- long enough to feel valuable
- short enough to use quickly
- distinct from the main book

## Recommended Identity

It should have its own identity, but remain clearly connected to the main book.

Recommended naming directions:

- *Health Fundamentals Companion Field Guide*
- *The Health Fundamentals Action Companion*
- *Health Fundamentals: First Steps Field Guide*

## Best Current Recommendation

Use:

**Health Fundamentals Companion Field Guide**

The phrase `quick-start` can still be used as a section inside the companion, but it should not define the whole asset.

## Delivery Strategy

### Direct Buyers

Best flow:

- buy on your site
- land on thank-you page
- get immediate access to the bonus resource

### Amazon Buyers

Best flow:

- find a code or private URL inside the book
- use that code or URL on your site
- access the companion after verification

## Verification Options

### Option 1: Access Code In The Book

Pros:

- simple
- buyer-only
- no fake lead magnet

Cons:

- code sharing is possible

### Option 2: Order Verification Form

Ask for:

- order number
- seller name
- purchase date

Pros:

- more controlled
- stronger buyer-only logic

Cons:

- more friction

## Best Practical Recommendation

Use:

- direct buyers: immediate thank-you page access
- Amazon buyers: inside-book code plus optional backup order verification

This is strong enough without becoming overly complex.

## Blender Guidance Without Brand Lock-In

Do not turn the companion into a brand-promotion document.

Instead, give readers a decision framework.

### What To Evaluate

- budget
- countertop space
- single-serve versus family-size use
- ease of cleaning
- replacement part availability
- jar material and size
- motor strength for their intended use
- warranty and durability

### Practical Buying Tiers

- basic: for simple softer blends and low budget
- mid-range: for regular daily use
- stronger tier: for heavier use, tougher ingredients, or family volume

### Helpful Guidance

- used or refurbished can be sensible if the machine is sturdy and parts are easy to replace
- readers do not need the most expensive blender to begin
- the right blender is the one they will actually use consistently

- earned
