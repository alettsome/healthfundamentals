# Business Workspace

This folder is for the book's thin business plan and related strategy documents.

It sits above the packaging materials and helps keep the project integrated rather than scattered.

## Relationship To Other Folders

- `Outline/` holds the manuscript thesis, mission, audience, and chapter structure.
- `Packaging/` holds market-facing assets such as cover text, positioning, and discoverability strategy.
- `Business/` ties those pieces together into one practical business view.

## Core Idea

The business plan does not need to be heavy or corporate.
It just needs to answer:

- what this book is
- who it is for
- why it matters
- how it is positioned
- how readers find it
- how the packaging and marketing should flow from the thesis
