# Brand Extension Ideas

## Purpose

Capture low-cost physical add-on ideas that fit the book's tone without turning the project into a merchandise business too early.

## Guiding Principle

The book and the companion should come first.

Physical add-ons should only be added if they:

- strengthen the reader experience
- stay minimal and useful
- feel aligned with the brand
- do not create heavy inventory stress

## Strongest Early Add-On Ideas

### 1. Minimal Health Journal

This is the strongest physical companion idea so far.

Why it fits:

- supports reflection and tracking
- matches the book's emphasis on observation
- feels useful rather than gimmicky
- can be designed in a clean premium-minimal way

Best direction:

- simple cover
- strong paper
- compact size
- light prompts, not heavy workbook pages
- premium feel without being expensive

### Companion Pen Direction

If the journal is paired with a pen, the pen should feel intentional and reusable.

Best direction:

- refillable pen
- simple and clean design
- comfortable for daily writing
- affordable enough to bundle without creating pricing pressure

Why this matters:

- it supports thoughts, notes, and health-journey reflection
- it adds practical value rather than novelty
- a refillable pen feels more durable and more thoughtful than a disposable one

Better naming directions:

- Health Fundamentals Journal
- Health Fundamentals Field Notes
- Foundations Journal

Possible bundle direction:

- journal plus refillable pen set

### 2. Minimal Mug

This can work, especially because tea culture is common in your environment.

Why it fits:

- simple
- affordable
- tied to daily ritual
- visually easy to keep minimal

Best use:

- later-stage add-on
- small-batch item
- optional gift bundle, not a core launch dependency

## What Not To Do Early

- do not launch many physical products at once
- do not tie up too much cash in inventory
- do not let merchandise distract from the book, site, and companion system

## Best Recommendation

If you choose one physical extension first, make it the journal.

Why:

- it supports the reader's health journey directly
- it feels more meaningful than generic merchandise
- it can connect naturally to the companion field guide

The mug can stay as a second-wave idea once the book and companion are established.
