# Title and Subtitle Options

## Working Direction

These options are designed to balance:

- conviction
- clarity
- discoverability
- affordability
- natural-health positioning

The strongest options usually pair a concept-led title with a more searchable subtitle.

## Best Current Strategic Candidates

If the goal is strongest balance between thesis and discoverability, my leading candidates are:

1. **Health Fundamentals**
   *A Natural, Affordable Guide to Whole-Food Nutrition, Detox, Daily Vitality, and Lasting Health*

2. **The Building Blocks of Health**
   *A Natural and Affordable Whole-Food Guide to Rebuilding Energy, Vitality, and Long-Term Wellness*

3. **Beyond Wellness Confusion**
   *How to Stop Chasing Fragments and Start Building Real Health*

## Recommendation Logic

- `Health Fundamentals` is the clearest and most category-friendly.
- `The Building Blocks of Health` ties directly to the book's internal philosophy.
- `Beyond Wellness Confusion` is emotionally strong and marketable, but would need a more searchable subtitle.

## Next Selection Strategy

Choose one of these depending on what you want the title to do most strongly:

- Use `Health Fundamentals` if you want maximum clarity and easier search alignment.
- Use `The Building Blocks of Health` if you want the book's internal framework to lead.
- Use `Beyond Wellness Confusion` if you want stronger emotional contrast and market identity.
