# Category Positioning

## Purpose

This document maps how the book should be positioned in bookstore and marketplace category language.

The goal is not only discoverability.
It is also expectation-setting.
The categories should help the right reader recognize the book quickly.

## Recommended Positioning Identity

This book sits at the intersection of:

- health fundamentals
- whole-food nutrition
- natural and holistic health
- practical wellness
- habit-based healthy living

It should not be positioned as:

- recipe-first cooking
- extreme detox
- purely academic nutrition
- biohacking optimization culture
- narrow supplement protocols

## Best Category Directions

These are the strongest category directions conceptually:

### 1. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Nutrition

Why it fits:

- the book teaches core nutrition and health foundations
- readers searching for practical nutrition help are likely to browse here

### 2. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Alternative & Holistic Medicine

Why it fits:

- the book has a clear natural-health orientation
- it speaks to readers who are already drawn to holistic or whole-body thinking

### 3. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Healthy Living

Why it fits:

- the book extends beyond nutrition into habits, detox, vitality, and sustainability
- it speaks to behavior and lifestyle, not just food chemistry

### 4. Self-Help / Healthy Living

Why it fits:

- the book includes agency, mindset, boundaries, accountability, and behavior change
- it is practical and reflective, not just informational

### 5. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Diet & Nutrition / General

Why it fits:

- broad enough to catch readers not yet looking specifically for holistic health
- useful if stronger natural-health categories feel too narrow

## Strategic Category Pairings

If only two or three categories can be emphasized, my recommendation is:

### Best Balanced Pairing

1. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Nutrition
2. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Alternative & Holistic Medicine
3. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Healthy Living

### Best Discoverability Pairing

1. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Nutrition
2. Self-Help / Healthy Living
3. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Diet & Nutrition / General

### Best Conviction-Led Pairing

1. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Alternative & Holistic Medicine
2. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Nutrition
3. Health, Fitness & Dieting / Healthy Living

## Shelf Positioning Statement

If someone asked what shelf this belongs on in plain language, I would describe it as:

`a practical natural-health and whole-food fundamentals book for readers who want affordable, sustainable wellness without fragmented supplement culture`

## Category Risks To Avoid

- Do not position it primarily as a cookbook.
- Do not position it primarily as a detox protocol book.
- Do not position it primarily as a technology book even though Chapter 11 matters.
- Do not let the AI chapter override the book's core shelf identity.

## Recommendation

The primary category identity should remain:

`natural, affordable, whole-food health fundamentals`

Everything else should support that, not replace it.
