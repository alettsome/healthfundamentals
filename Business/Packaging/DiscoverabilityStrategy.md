# Discoverability Strategy

## Goal

Help the book become easier to find in search and marketplace systems without making the language fake, trend-chasing, or disconnected from the manuscript.

## Principle

Do not replace the thesis with keyword stuffing.
Instead, translate the thesis into the kinds of phrases readers actually use when they search.

## Strategic Approach

### 1. Preserve The Conviction

The book remains:

- natural-health oriented
- whole-food centered
- affordability aware
- anti-fragmentation in its worldview
- practical rather than abstract

### 2. Translate Into Search Language

Use reader-facing language that overlaps with search behavior:

- affordable natural health
- whole food healing
- health fundamentals
- mineral and vitamin deficiency support
- detox your life
- healthy smoothies
- simple healing foods
- natural health for beginners
- chronic illness nutrition support
- gut and nervous system health
- sustainable healthy habits
- wellness without supplements

### 3. Use A Layered Packaging Model

Different parts of the package should do different jobs:

- Title: memorable and conceptually strong
- Subtitle: clear, searchable, benefit-driven
- Back cover: emotional resonance plus practical promise
- Marketplace description: fuller keyword overlap without sounding robotic
- Categories and metadata: discoverability support

### 4. Keep The Right Reader In View

The ideal reader is not searching for academic phrasing.
They are often searching from fatigue, confusion, budget pressure, chronic symptoms, frustration with modern wellness, or desire for a more natural way.

## Recommended Next Packaging Tasks

1. Generate title and subtitle directions
2. Build a keyword bank based on likely reader searches
3. Draft back cover and marketplace copy
4. Review all packaging language against the thesis so it stays honest
