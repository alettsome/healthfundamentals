# Packaging Workspace

This folder is for the book's market-facing materials rather than manuscript chapters.

Use it to keep packaging decisions separate from the actual chapter drafts.

## What Belongs Here

- Front cover text
- Back cover text
- Positioning and discoverability strategy
- Title and subtitle options
- Category and keyword strategy
- Marketplace-facing copy

## Core Strategy Sources

These documents remain the foundation for packaging decisions:

- `Outline/BookThesis.md`
- `Outline/MissionVisionAudience.md`
- `Outline/Outline1.md`
- `Business/ThinBusinessPlan.md`

## Recommended Workflow

1. Finalize manuscript direction and chapter progression.
2. Clarify the book's thesis, audience, and promise.
3. Tie the packaging to the thin business plan.
4. Build the positioning pack.
5. Draft front cover and back cover text.
6. Refine title, subtitle, keywords, and description together so they stay aligned.
