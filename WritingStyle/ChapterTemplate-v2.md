# Chapter Template v2

This is the canonical chapter structure for the manuscript.

It is designed to support the actual voice of the book:
- practical and compassionate
- story-supported rather than purely academic
- structured enough for consistency without forcing every section to sound identical

## Required Chapter Anchors

Every chapter should include these anchor sections in this order:

## Introduction
- Define the chapter's focus clearly.
- Tell the reader why this chapter matters now.
- Set up the promise of the chapter in reader-friendly language.

## [Chapter-Specific Core Sections]
- Add one or more `##` sections tailored to the chapter topic.
- These are the main teaching sections for the chapter.
- They do not need to reuse Chapter 1 headings like `Water` or `Minerals`.
- Historical context can appear here when it genuinely strengthens the chapter.
  When used, it can work well as a short direct-prose note after the introduction and before the Judy/Alex narration begins.

## Integration: The Missing Link
- Connect the main ideas instead of leaving them isolated.
- Show how the concepts work together in real life.
- Transition from explanation to application.

### [Practical Subsection 1]
### [Practical Subsection 2]
### [Practical Subsection 3]
- Include at least one practical `###` subsection between Integration and the personal plan.
- Two or three is often ideal.
- Use recipes, routines, checklists, examples, or decision-making frameworks.

## Your Personal Food Swap Plan
- Give the reader a simple action step or self-guided exercise.
- Keep it practical, not abstract.
- Make the chapter usable immediately.

## Conclusion, Ending Summary, Closing Reflection, or Key Takeaways (recommended)
- Close the chapter with emotional and conceptual closure.
- Reinforce momentum and reader confidence.
- Transition naturally into the next chapter when helpful.

## References or Notes (optional)
- Include studies, source notes, or clarifications when the chapter relies on them.

---

## Voice Rules

These are chapter-level expectations, not rigid per-section requirements:

- Use Judy, Alex, Narrator, affirmations, and inner voice where they genuinely help.
- Do not force every element into every single section.
- Main teaching sections should usually feel alive, supportive, and human.
- Dialogue, narrator commentary, and reflective inner voice should serve clarity, not become a checklist.

## Robustness Rules

- The chapter must not rely on a single giant undifferentiated body section.
- The structure should move from orientation, to teaching, to integration, to action.
- Historical context is optional and can be woven in rather than isolated as a mandatory heading.
- The chapter should feel complete even if references are omitted.
- The closing section is strongly recommended because it improves reader confidence and continuity.
