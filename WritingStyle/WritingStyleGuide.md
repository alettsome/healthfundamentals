# Writing Style Guide

## Purpose
This document defines the preferred writing style for all content in this project. It ensures consistency, clarity, and engagement across all chapters and sections.

Canonical structure note:
- `ChapterTemplate-v2.md` is the current source of truth for chapter structure.
- Older templates in this folder are reference material, not the primary structural standard.

## Tone and Voice
- **Engaging and Relatable**: Use a conversational tone that connects with the reader.
- **Empowering**: Focus on hope, agency, and practical next steps.
- **Balanced**: Combine storytelling with factual information supported by credible references.
- **Grounded**: Be warm and human without becoming vague, preachy, or repetitive.

## Structure
- **Hybrid Approach**: Blend narrative paragraphs with occasional bullet points for clarity.
- **Historical Contrast**: Historical context is welcome when it deepens the chapter, but it does not need its own mandatory heading.
- **Incorporate Studies**: Reference recent and relevant studies to validate claims and provide depth.
- **Flow**: Move from orientation, to teaching, to integration, to action, to closure.

## Chapter Structure
Each chapter should follow the canonical structure in `ChapterTemplate-v2.md`.
In practical terms, that means:

1. **Introduction**:
   - Clearly define the chapter's subject and its importance.
   - Tie the introduction explicitly to the chapter title and focus.

2. **Chapter-Specific Core Sections**:
   - Use one or more `##` sections tailored to the chapter topic.
   - These sections carry the main explanations, examples, contrasts, and story material.
   - Historical context can be woven in here when helpful.

3. **Integration and Action**:
   - Use `Integration: The Missing Link` to connect the chapter's ideas.
   - Follow with practical `###` subsections.
   - End the applied portion with `Your Personal Food Swap Plan`.

4. **Closing Section**:
   - Strongly prefer ending with `Conclusion`, `Ending Summary`, `Closing Reflection`, or `Key Takeaways`.
   - The closing should reinforce momentum and help the reader leave the chapter with clarity.

5. **References or Notes**:
   - Optional, but include them when the chapter relies on studies, citations, or clarifications.

## Key Elements
1. **Chapter-Specific Introductions**:
   - Each chapter introduction should be explicitly tied to the chapter title and focus.
   - Avoid letting the introduction drift into a generic essay.

2. **Historical Context**:
   - Use it when it meaningfully supports the chapter.
   - It can appear as a dedicated passage or be woven into a core section.
   - It should support the chapter's argument, not delay it.

3. **Hope and Empowerment**:
   - Show readers how they can reclaim their health despite modern challenges.
   - Focus on progress, practicality, and achievable next steps.

4. **Engagement**:
   - Use anecdotes, vivid descriptions, and relatable examples.
   - Avoid overly technical language; explain concepts in simple terms.

5. **Credible References**:
   - Incorporate references to studies, research, or historical records when claims need support.
   - Use references to strengthen clarity, not to overwhelm the reader.

## Voice Devices
- Judy, Alex, Narrator, affirmations, and inner voice are part of the book's toolkit.
- Use them consistently across a chapter, but do not force every element into every section.
- The goal is living, supportive prose, not a mechanical checklist.

## Formatting
- **Headings**: Use clear and descriptive headings to organize content.
- **Paragraphs**: Keep paragraphs concise and focused.
- **Bullet Points**: Use sparingly for summarizing key points or providing quick tips.
- **Citations**: Include in-text citations or footnotes for referenced studies.
