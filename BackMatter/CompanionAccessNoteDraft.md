# Companion Access Note

Thank you for reading *Health Fundamentals*.

If this book has helped you think more clearly about health, you do not have to stop here.

There is also a practical buyer companion created to help you put the book into daily life:

**Health Fundamentals Companion Field Guide**

This companion is designed to help you move from understanding into action.
It includes practical support such as:

- first food swaps
- tight-week buying guidance
- produce washing and storage tips
- water and filter basics
- blender-building principles
- blender-buying guidance
- simple reset pages for hard weeks

## How To Access The Companion

### If You Bought Directly From My Website

You should receive access through your purchase confirmation or thank-you page.

### If You Bought Through Amazon Or Another Retailer

Visit:

`yourdomain.com/companion`

or scan the QR code provided in this book.

When prompted, enter the access word or code printed in your copy.

## Why The Companion Exists

The book gives you the framework.
The companion helps you apply that framework more easily in real life.

It is not meant to replace the book.
It is meant to help you use it.

## A Note On Simplicity

This companion was created with the same spirit as the book:

- practical, not theatrical
- useful, not cluttered
- supportive, not overwhelming

If you feel like you need to begin more simply, that is fine.
Use one page.
Use one idea.
Use one better decision and build from there.

## Keep In Touch With The Book's Home Base

Your website is the living home for companion access, future updates, and any added support material connected to the book.

Use the space in a way that serves you.
